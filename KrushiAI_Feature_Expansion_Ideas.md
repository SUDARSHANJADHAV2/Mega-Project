## 🚀 **Technology Implementation Suggestions**

### **APIs to Integrate**
- **Weather APIs**: OpenWeatherMap, AccuWeather
- **Market Data**: AGMARKNET, commodity exchanges
- **Satellite Data**: NASA, ESA for crop monitoring
- **Soil Data**: Government soil health databases
- **Government Schemes**: Direct Benefit Transfer APIs

### **AI/ML Enhancement Areas**
- **Computer Vision**: Extend to pest/weed detection
- **Time Series**: Weather and price forecasting
- **Recommendation Systems**: Personalized suggestions
- **Natural Language Processing**: Voice commands, chatbots
- **Predictive Analytics**: Yield and risk forecasting

### **Mobile Development**
- **React Native** or **Flutter** for cross-platform
- **Offline Data Synchronization**
- **Camera Integration** for image capture
- **GPS Services** for location-based features
